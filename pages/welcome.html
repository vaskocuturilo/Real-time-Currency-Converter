✍️ Method 1: Manual Add (Instant)

Have a specific name in mind? Add it directly.

  1. Open the extension popup by clicking the floating widget in the lower right corner.
  2. Type the amount with currency name or icon into the input field.
  3. Currency conversion will be performed automatically.

✏️ Method 2: Highlighting Mode (Visual)

Highlight any amount on any webpage to see the conversion.

  1. Find an amount with currency on the browser page and highlight it.
  2. A tooltip will appear showing the converted amount instantly.

📂 Extension supports all currencies in the world

Works with ranges, suffixes, and formats like:

  1. 1000 EURO -> 1000€
  2. 1K - 2K € -> от 1 млн до 2 млн ₽
