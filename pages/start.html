Pin Your Extension for Quick Access

Follow these two quick steps to pin the currency converter to your browser toolbar.

1

Click the Extensions Puzzle Icon

Look at the top-right corner of your Chrome browser toolbar and click the puzzle piece icon 🧩 to open your installed extensions list.

2

Find and Click the Pin Icon

Locate Real-Time Currency Converter in the drop-down menu and click the **Pin icon** 📌 next to it so it stays visible on your toolbar.
